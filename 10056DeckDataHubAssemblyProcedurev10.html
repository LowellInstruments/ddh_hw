General Workmanship Standards

Follow standard ESD precautions:  ESD mat and wrist strap.

Torque all metal M2.5 hardware to 20 cNm

Cut all zip ties flush.

Wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment.

Subassemblies

10059 - Push Button Harness  

  • Cut the 4-pin cable (10033)  to 11” overall and strip back ½” insulation
  • Twist one lead from each switch (3X 10032) with the green (GROUND) lead.
  • Twist each remaining switch-lead with a jumper wire (Red/Blk/Blue)
  • Tin each connection
  • Cover each connection with a 1” piece of adhesive-lined heat-shrink (10031).
  • Heat each piece of heat-shrink with the heat gun and pinch the end closed with pliers
  • Label each switch per the schematic

10075 - Power Button Harness

  • Confirm power-button (10022) wire length (stock is 10” overall length)
  • Check switch harness pinout
  • Remove blue wire by poking contact with a screwdriver (if present)
  • Strip wires back by ¼”
  • Crimp ring terminals (10023) on 4 wires

  • Check switch harness pinout as shown. Note:  housing color may vary.

10078 - Fuse Harness

  • Cut wire of fuse-holder (10051) to form lengths of 4” on left side, 3” on right
  • Strip wires back by ¼”
  • Crimp ring terminals (10023) on each end
  • Install fuse (10052) in fuse-holder

10139 - Power Cable, Internal  

  • Twist black wire (10062) and red wire (10061) together using a cordless drill.  Spin until the wires are twisted 2x/inch.
  • Cut the twisted wire into 5” lengths
  • Strip insulation from ¼” each end
  • Crimp ring terminals (10023) onto both ends as shown.
  • Overall length should be 6”

10082 - Bluetooth Adapter

10097 - Mounting Base with Adhesive

  • Apply double-sided tape-disc (10106)  to the underside of the mounting base (10007).

10122 - Power Input Cable

  • Slide the connector nut onto the connector end

10114 - AC Adapter with Inline Power Connector

  • Cut off the AD/DC barrel plug and ferrite 
  • Cut off ¼” from the power-connector wires (the tinned part)
  • Cut back the both cable jackets by 1.25”
  • Strip wire insulation back by ¼” on both cables
  • Tin each end of the cables (4 wires total)
  • Use a drop of flux and leaded solder

  • Slide the heatshrink pieces over the AC adapter cable as shown
  • Lap-splice the red and black wires

  • Shrink the ⅛ diameter heatshrink over the joints
  • Slide the first piece of ¼” diameter heatshrink over the combined joint and shrink.
  • Slide the ¼” heat-shrink centered over the joint and shrink

10116 - Two Pin Power Plug Assembly

  • Splice 4.5” of 18AWG red and black wire onto the connector (male pins) whip:
  • Strip wire insulation back by ¼” on all wires (6 ends)
  • Tin the two connector wires and one end of the red/black wires.
  • Use a drop of liquid flux and leaded-solder
  • Lap splice the red and black wires to the connector
  • Apply a piece of ⅛ x 3/4” heatshrink over both splices
  • Apply a ¼” x 1” piece of adhesive-lined heat-shrink over the cable jacket and splices
  • Add a 16mm cable gland (10018) as shown.  Do not tighten.
  • Crimp ring terminals on the red/black wires

10131 - DC Power Input Cable

  • Using side-cutters, cut back 3” of the white cable jacket
  • Using the rotating jacket cutter, cut back 1.5” of the black cable jacket
  • Cut off the soldered ends of the black cable (approx ¼”)
  • Strip wire insulation back by ¾” on both cables
  • Slide a 1/4 x ¾” piece of heatshrink onto the longer (white cable) wires
  • Slide a 7” piece of ⅜” followed by a 4” piece of ¼” heatshrink onto the white cable
  • Hook splice the red and black wires as-shown.  
  • Use the “Third-hand” to do this neatly.
  • Use a drop of flux on each wire end
  • Shrink the ⅛” heatshrink over the hook splices
  • Shrink the shorter piece of ¼” heat shrink over the splices
  • Shrink the longer piece of ¼” heat shrink overlapping both cable jackets.
  • Cut back 2” of cable jacket on the free end of the white cable
  • Coil the cable

10141 - USB-C Pi-Power Cable

  • Cut the USB-C power-only cable (10134) to an overall length of 16”
  • Strip back 1” of the cable jacket
  • Strip ¼” of insulation from each wire
  • Crimp a wire ferrule (10129) onto each wire

10142 - USB-Micro Screen Power Cable

  • Cut the USB-micro cable (10140) to an overall length of 16”
  • Strip back 1” of the cable jacket
  • Strip ¼” of insulation from each wire
  • Shrink a ¾” length of ⅛ heatshrink over the end of the cable jacket
  • Crimp a wire ferrule (10129) onto each wire

Lower Enclosure Assembly

Swivel Mount Installation

  • Grease the #10-32 x 1” seal-screw (10002) threads with a dab of red grease (10066)
  • Install a seal-screw from the inside of the enclosure, pass through the mount with adhesive (10097)
  • Align the mount approximately vertical (hex flats) and stick to the enclosure
  • Thread-on a #10 nylock nut (10003) using a ⅜” wrench and extended #2 Phillips drive.  Use the wrench until the nut is engaged in the mount. (Torque to 12 in-lb)
  • Repeat for the second side

Install Power Switch

  • Using a ¾” or 19mm wrench, install the power switch as shown.

Terminal Block

  • Assemble terminal block/plate using:  
  • (10067) terminal block mount
  • (10021) terminal block
  • (10026) 6-32 screw x2  (6 in-lbs)
  • (10025) 6-32 nylock nut x2

  • Remove all terminal-screws
  • Connect power-switch harness (10075).  Torque terminal-screws to 9 in-lb.  
  • Install in lower-left corner of enclosure with 3x self tapping screws (12 in-lb).  

Route wires underneath and to the right.

  • Connect fuse subassembly (10078)
  • Attach the power-switch harness to the switch  

Install Power Input Cable

  • Install the cable into the lower enclosure and tighten the panel-nut
  • Zip-tie the red and black wires to the right slot on the terminal-block-bracket
  • Connect to the terminal block as usual
  • Tighten the cable gland nut

Install DC-DC Converter

  • Secure the DC-DC converter (10132) and bracket (10133) to the lower right of the enclosure using 4x self-tapping screws.
  • Attach the two ring terminals from the terminal block wiring to the “12/24V Input” screw terminals
  • Attach the red/black wires from the Screen and Pi power cables to the Output terminals.

Note:  Red is “+”, Black is “-“

  • Secure all three wire bundles to the terminal-block bracket using a zip-tie

Vent, Blank-plugs

  • Install 16mm blank-plug (10020) on the right side using a 22m wrench
  • Install vent plug (10019) on the bottom side using a 27mm wrench
  • Install larger cable gland (10083) plugged with a short piece of o-ring cord (????)
  • Clean enclosure with canned-air

Upper Enclosure Assembly

Screen Installation

  • Inspect screen (10037)  for damage.  Do not remove the protective film.
  • Test fit screen in the face cutout.  The surface of the screen should be flush with the enclosure.  Note:  The screen is not symmetrical and will fit in only one orientation:  with the flex-cable connector facing the switch holes on the enclosure.

  • Clean the machined surfaces of the screen cutout with isopropyl alcohol and paper towels.  Use a new part of the paper towel for each area.  Allow to dry for 10 minutes
  • Apply a 1” strip of flash-tape around the perimeter of the screen cutout.  Trim the screen opening with a razor knife.

  • Apply a continuous bead of RTV (P/N) around the perimeter of the screen cutout.  

  • Note screen orientation relative to three case holes. The flex-cable connector faces the button holes on the enclosure.
  • Lower the screen into position
  • Press gently around the perimeter of the screen to squeeze some RTV out from the gap.

  • Allow 48 hours to cure
  • Using a razor-scraper, cut through the bead of RTV, then remove the blue masking tape.  Do not remove the screen-protection film.

Screen Preparation

  • Inspect hardware securing LCD control board to the screen.  Confirm M2.5 x 12mm.  
  • Replace the brass standoff in the lower-right with a nylon 12mm standoff (10099).  Note:  Cut the male thread in half using flush-cutters.  
  • Remove M2.5 pan head screws from these standoffs if present and save.  
  • Apply a dot of RTV on either side of the small flex-cable.
  • Apply a dot of RTV on the underside of the large flex cable where it wraps around the metal screen backing.  (both sides)

LCD Clamp Installation

Wear gloves and eye-protection when working with the adhesive.  The adhesive has a 20 minute working time.  

  • Orient the lid assembly with the button-holes on the right
  • Test-fit the left and right screen brackets.  Remove and set aside.  
  • Using the adhesive dispenser, cartridge, plunger, and mixing tips - apply adhesive as shown:

  • Place LCD Clamps (10028, 10029)  in the lid as shown.  Note orientation.  Press down gently to fully seat the clamps.  Some adhesive should ooze out.

  • Add a plastic spacer to each corner

  • Place a lower-enclosure half on the 4 plastic spacers
  • Add a 10 pound weight to the lower enclosure.

  • Allow 12 hours to cure

Screen-Connection

  • Pull out the locking tabs on the flex-cable connector.
  • Insert the flex cable (10109) fully, shiny-side up,  and push both locking tabs back in.

  • Secure the flex cable to the screen-bracket using 1 piece of VHB tape underneath, and 1 piece on-top.  Do not remove backing film from the top piece yet.  

Button Installation

  • Note the three switch designations:

  • Install three labeled switches through the case holes.  Place a rubber washer on the outside and secure them with the included nut.  Tighten by hand
  • Secure the harness to the screen bracket with 4 zip-ties

Raspberry Pi Install

  • Install the configured micro-SD Card (10143)
  • Attach the Raspberry-Pi (10038) to the screen control board.  Note orientation of the flex-connector.
  • Secure with 4x (10085)  M2.5 X 15MM standoffs
  • Torque to 20 cNm
  • Loosen locking tabs, insert the flex cable from the screen to the Pi, push tabs back in.
  • Fold the flex cable down onto the VHB strip (remove backing)
  • Attach the 4-pin button connector to the header pins as shown.  Bend outward to clear the next board in the stack and secure the wires to the nearest standoff with a zip-tie.
  • Route the button wires far from the internal GPS antenna and secure to the LCD bracket with a zip tie.  

4G Hat Install

  • Install the 4G module (10041) onto the 4G Hat (10040)
  • Ensure that both locking tabs have clicked into place
  • Add this board to the stack as shown
  • Secure with 4x m2.5 Screws and loctite
  • Connect the USB Cable (10044) between the 4G hat and the Pi. Use the lower-outboard port

4G Antennas

  • For each antenna, remove ½ of the backing and position antenna.  Peel off remaining backing and press the antenna against the enclosure.  Note locations as shown.  
  • Connect the top antenna to the “DIV” connector on the 4G hat.
  • Connect the side antenna to the “Main” connector on the 4G hat.
  • Secure micro-coax to the screen brackets with 1 zip tie 10027
  • Secure micro-coax to the closest standoff with 1 zip tie 10027

(If) Installing External GPS Antenna

Requires:  Upper Enclosure modified with extra through-hole

      10113 BNC to UFL Cable Assembly

Install and secure the cable starting at the UFL connector end on the SixFab module. (This reduces twists in the cable between the UFL and panel-mount end)

Tighten the external nut - Gently - using a ⅝ and 11/16 wrench

(Else) Installing Internal GPS Antenna

  • Attach the GPS antenna to the bottom side - aligning the coax cable with the edge of the screen bracket.  
  • Connect this antenna to the “GNSS” connector on the 4G Hat.
  • Secure the cable to the adjacent standoff (1 layer down) with a tie-wrap

External Bluetooth Adapter

  • Connect the Bluetooth adapter to the lower-inboard USB port
  • Secure with a zip-tie through the screen bracket

Connecting Enclosures

  • Connect the USB-Micro cable to the screen control board (bottom of the stack
  • Connect the USB-C cable to the Pi input connector (middle of the stack)
  • Secure both cables to the LCD screen brack with a zip-tie.

Initial Power-up Check

  • Plug in the AC adapter
  • Push the power-button
  • Check for LED activity on:
  • UPS - initially 1 light, more as it charges
  • SSD activity
  • Green/Red on Raspberry Pi
  • Confirm splash screen or Raspbian OS window on LCD.
  • Test touchscreen response by moving an icon
  • Menu -> shutdown

  • Wipe with IPA
  • Use the recorded FCC IDs
  • Print a label and apply here:

Graphic Overlay

  • Remove the film from the screen
  • Check for debris, glue, etc.. around the perimeter of the enclosure cutout and screen
  • Clean the perimeter of the cutout and screen with IPA.  Clean fingers as well before proceeding.  
  • Remove the center rectangle from the overlay sheet (10009)
  • Fold back the overlay backing slightly past the cutout at the top of the label
  • Align the overlay with the top of the cutout (power cable on left side)
  • Peel the backing away as you lower the label farther
  • Press around the perimeter of the overlay

Configuration

(If) Connecting and External Bluetooth cable and antenna

  • Install the large-ID cable gland in the lower enclosure.  (Else a plug if not using)
  • Feed the external antenna cable through the cable gland, connect to the Adapter

  • Gently pull the antenna cable through the gland as you lower the top enclosure
  • Tighten the cable gland nut

(Else) If not using the external Bluetooth Cable and antenna:

  • Install a 16mm hole-plug (10020) into the upper right hole of the enclosure

Complete Assembly

  • Attach top of enclosure to the bottom using 4x captive screws.  Note orientation of screen vs. power cable entry.  Thread screws only through top half
  • Attach 2x support legs(10008) using 2x thumb-nuts (10005) and 2x fender washers (10004)
  • Set orientation as shown for further configuration and testing

  • Remove protective film from base plate (side without countersunk holes)
  • Secure support legs to rectangular base (10048) using 2x ¼-20 flat-head screws (10071) ,2x washers (10072), and 2x Nylock nuts (10073).  Lubricate screws with red grease before assembly.
  • Torque to ~14 in-lbs
